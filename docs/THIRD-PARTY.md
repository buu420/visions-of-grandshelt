The game-local bootstrap uses Reloaded-II 1.30.1. The Windows ZIP has its license at `ResonanceAccess/LICENSES/Reloaded-II-LICENSE.txt`. The corresponding [Reloaded-II source revision](https://github.com/Reloaded-Project/Reloaded-II/tree/8ea7483135a7e369052252bffb561877538f993f) and the mod's adapter source are available from the release and this repository.

Screen reader output uses [Prism 0.18.3](https://github.com/ethindp/prism/tree/v0.18.3). Prism's notices and dependency license texts are included in the ZIP. The reader incorporates [MinHook 1.3.4](https://github.com/TsudaKageyu/minhook/tree/c3fcafdc10146beb5919319d0683e44e3c30d537) and the patch under `cmake`. Its license is included in the ZIP.

The positional audio banks use the [CIPIC HRTF database](https://www.ece.ucdavis.edu/cipic/spatial-sound/hrtf-data/), whose notice is included in the ZIP and under `licenses`. NPC and interactable cues use effects supplied by buu420 for Echos of Gaia. See [field proximity sounds](../assets/beacon/field-cues.md) for the cue identities. The author supplied the voice for the original accessibility narration included in the release.
